## Logo and Trademark Policy

The brand name AXERP and the logo are trademarks of Axina Group Inc.

### Introduction

Axina Group Inc. owns and oversees the trademarks for the AXERP name and logos. We have developed this trademark usage policy with the following goals in mind:

- We’d like to make it easy for anyone to use the AXERP name or logo for community-oriented efforts that help spread and improve AXERP.
- We’d like to make it clear how AXERP-related businesses and projects can (and cannot) use the AXERP name and logo.
- We’d like to make it hard for anyone to use the AXERP name and logo to unfairly profit from, trick or confuse people who are looking for official AXERP resources.

### AXERP Trademark Usage Policy

Permission from Axina Group Inc. is required to use the AXERP name or logo as part of any project, product, service, domain or company name.

We will grant permission to use the AXERP name and logo for projects that meet the following criteria:

- The primary purpose of your project is to promote the spread and improvement of the AXERP software.
- Your project is non-commercial in nature (it can make money to cover its costs or contribute to non-profit entities, but it cannot be run as a for-profit project or business).
Your project neither promotes nor is associated with entities that currently fail to comply with the license under which AXERP is distributed.
- If your project meets these criteria, you will be permitted to use the AXERP name and logo to promote your project in any way you see fit with one exception: Please do not use AXERP as part of a domain name.

Use of the AXERP name and logo is additionally allowed in the following situations:

All other AXERP-related businesses or projects can use the AXERP name and logo to refer to and explain their services, but they cannot use them as part of a product, project, service, domain, or company name and they cannot use them in any way that suggests an affiliation with or endorsement by AXERP or Axina Group Inc. or the AXERP project. For example, a consulting company can describe its business as “123 Web Services, offering AXERP consulting for small businesses,” but cannot call its business “The AXERP Consulting Company.”

Similarly, it’s OK to use the AXERP logo as part of a page that describes your products or services, but it is not OK to use it as part of your company or product logo or branding itself. Under no circumstances is it permitted to use AXERP as part of a top-level domain name.

We do not allow the use of the trademark in advertising, including AdSense/AdWords.

Please note that it is not the goal of this policy to limit commercial activity around AXERP. We encourage AXERP-based businesses, and we would love to see hundreds of them.

When in doubt about your use of the AXERP name or logo, please contact Axina Group Inc. for clarification.
